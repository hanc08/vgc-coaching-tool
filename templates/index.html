DartsVG Pokemon Practice Tool

Hello! My name is Christopher Han, and I work as a Pokemon VGC coach to help other players grow and improve.

It's often difficult for newer VGC players to determine the correct moves in the last 1-2 turns of a game. This website is a tool (designed by myself) which aims to provide specific practice and feedback in different VGC scenarios to my students!

The tool is currently reserved for my coaching students. If you would like to work with me, feel free to reach out through my socials!
