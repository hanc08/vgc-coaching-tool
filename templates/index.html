DartsVG Pokemon Practice Tool

Hello! My name is Christopher Han, and I work as a Pokemon VGC coach to help other players grow and improve.

It's often difficult for newer VGC players to determine the correct moves in the last 1-2 turns of a game. This website is a tool (designed by myself) which aims to provide specific practice and feedback in different VGC scenarios!

If you have any questions or would like to submit level ideas, don't be afraid to reach out through social media!
